I'm a Senior Applied Scientist at the [Automated Reasoning Group](https://aws.amazon.com/security/provable-security/) at AWS. My research combines automated reasoning and neuro-symbolic AI to improve LLM-agent reliability, with soundness as the north star. I spent the earlier part of my career building model checkers and verifiers for C, C++, and Rust — tools like [CBMC](https://github.com/diffblue/cbmc), [ESBMC](https://github.com/esbmc/esbmc), and [Kani](https://github.com/model-checking/kani) — and that foundation in formal methods now shapes how I think about making AI agents trustworthy.
